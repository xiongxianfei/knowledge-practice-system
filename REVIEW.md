# Content and usability review

## Key takeaway
Structural checks are necessary aids; local understanding, faithful compression and domain validity remain substantive judgments.

## Summary
The release retained useful source-package content and reviewed the publication as an integrated knowledge system. The review focused on knowledge-role boundaries, inline Practice execution, source-to-claim scope and the separation of identity, sequence and navigation. No independent reader or domain-expert usability study was performed.

## Review questions used
A Concept needs a meaningful distinction and local explanation. A Principle must remain a declarative relationship beyond a single rule. A Model includes its actual representation and assumptions. A Method has a rationale, inputs, executable steps, observations and limits. A Practice makes the next action and fallback understandable with links hidden. A Reference explains what was inspected and what it cannot establish.

## Integration focus
The domain-building and use Practices were checked against the classification, explanatory-Principle, source-synthesis and local-completeness Methods. Navigation and compatibility are explicit operating conventions; they are not offered as empirical Principles. The new profile uses one source edition.

## Remaining uncertainty
A concise summary can still omit an important condition. A well-formed procedural file can be unsuitable for a reader or context. A reference with a correct URL may contain incomplete or disputed support. Read the evidence and limits before relying on consequential conclusions; obtain domain-specific expertise where needed.

## Practical acceptance check
Choose one real bounded task, hide outgoing links, and ask whether the reader can explain the claim or perform the permitted operation without guessing critical prerequisites. Then restore links and inspect fidelity. Record actual difficulties and observations; do not prefill an acceptance result. [Authoring](AUTHORING.md) and [Checks](CHECKS.md) separate that substantive review from machine validation.
