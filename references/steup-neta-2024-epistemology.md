# Epistemology

## Key takeaway

Knowledge, justification and knowing how raise distinct questions with competing philosophical accounts.

## Summary

This selected reading supports foundational topics in the [subject map](../docs/subject-map.md), without adopting one complete epistemology.

## Identity and inspection

Matthias Steup and Ram Neta, *Epistemology*, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, revised 2024-10-26. [Publication](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology/). The HTML passages listed in metadata were inspected on 2026-10-14.

## Located contribution

Section 2.2 presents disagreement about knowing how and knowing that. Section 2.3 discusses factual knowledge, belief, truth, justification and the Gettier problem. The opening of section 3 distinguishes justification from a guarantee of knowledge.

## Limits

This is a scholarly survey, not an empirical study. The cited philosophical works were not independently inspected. It does not define KPS roles or settle the disagreements described. Dedicated Core explanations remain to be developed.
