# Knowledge and reasoning

## Key takeaway

Learning includes knowledge integration and expertise as well as retention.

## Summary

This chapter supplies a broader learning perspective for the [body of knowledge outline](../docs/body-of-knowledge-outline.md).

## Identity and inspection

National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, *How People Learn II: Learners, Contexts, and Cultures* (2018), chapter 5, pp. 85–108. [Publisher text](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/24783/chapter/7). Selected HTML passages listed in metadata were inspected on 2026-10-14.

## Located contribution

Pages 85–86 introduce integration; pp. 90–92 discuss expertise. Pages 97–98 and 101–102 distinguish retention strategies from activities for understanding. The conclusions on pp. 106–107 retain learner, material and contextual conditions.

## Limits

The full chapter, figures and underlying studies were not independently audited. The report is a synthesis, not an additional independent experiment for each cited finding. It does not establish a KPS curriculum or a universal learning schedule. Existing Core claims retain their own evidence limits.
