# Complex images

## Key takeaway

The guidance pairs an identifying text alternative with a fuller account of essential image information. It supports retaining the relationships or data in nearby text rather than relying on visual appearance alone.

## Summary

This record identifies a specific contribution to the steps-first authoring design. It does not establish the effectiveness of KPS or require the same presentation in every stage.
