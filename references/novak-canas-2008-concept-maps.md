# Concept maps and their construction

## Key takeaway

The authors describe concept maps through concepts, explicit linking phrases, focus questions and cross-links.

## Summary

This record supports the representation and reconstruction topics in the [body of knowledge outline](../docs/body-of-knowledge-outline.md). It does not establish the effectiveness of KPS.

## Identity and inspected extent

Joseph D. Novak and Alberto J. Cañas, IHMC, *The Theory Underlying Concept Maps and How to Construct and Use Them*, Technical Report 2006-01, revision 2008-01. [Author publication](https://cmap.ihmc.us/docs/theory-of-concept-maps).

Inspected on 2026-10-13: HTML Introduction and Constructing Good Concept Maps. Figures and underlying empirical studies were not inspected.

## Located contribution

Introduction distinguishes concepts from propositions formed through meaningful linking phrases. It describes contextual hierarchy and relationships across map regions. Constructing Good Concept Maps recommends a bounded focus question, selecting relevant concepts and revising relationships.

## Limits and KPS use
