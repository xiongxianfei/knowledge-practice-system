# Practical reason

## Key takeaway

Deliberation concerns reasons for action, including questions about ends and means.

## Summary

The entry supplies selected distinctions for practical reasoning in the [body of knowledge outline](../docs/body-of-knowledge-outline.md).

## Identity and inspection

R. Jay Wallace and Benjamin Kiesewetter, *Practical Reason*, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, revised 2024-07-31. [Publication](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/practical-reason/). The HTML passages listed in metadata were inspected on 2026-10-14.

## Located contribution

Section 1 contrasts reasoning about action with theoretical reasoning. The selected section 4 discussion distinguishes means and ends coherence from questions about which ends merit pursuit.
