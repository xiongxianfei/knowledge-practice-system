# How to guides

## Key takeaway

Diataxis describes task-oriented instructions and acknowledges branching paths. Its strict how-to guidance separates explanation from action. KPS adopts task orientation but deliberately retains essential local explanations for its declared reader.

## Summary

This record identifies a specific contribution to the steps-first authoring design. It does not establish the effectiveness of KPS or require the same presentation in every stage.
