## Summary

This study revisits a particular retrieval-versus-mapping comparison by varying additional memorization and instructions. Its verbatim and inference outcomes need separate treatment. It challenges a broad interpretation of an earlier comparison, not every retrieval finding.

## Verbatim performance under the revised comparison

The study reports no statistically significant retrieval advantage on verbatim questions once additional memorization is included in the mapping comparison. This is a condition-specific result.

**Locator:** Results and Discussion, verbatim-question analysis.

## Inference outcomes and their limited measurement

The additional-memorization comparison did not show a significant retrieval advantage on verbatim questions. Inference results were different, but the authors caution that only two inference questions were used.

**Locator:** Results and Discussion, especially sections 3 and 4.

## What this source does not establish

This tests a particular comparison. It neither establishes that retrieval is useless nor validates KPS.
