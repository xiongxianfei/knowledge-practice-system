# Application and revision model

## Key takeaway

Connect knowledge to real use while preserving the distinction between results and interpretation.

## Summary

Start with a documented action and expected result, record actual use, then decide whether the issue concerns the wording, the assumption, the procedure or the task context. A good result does not require revising every object.

## Local explanatory basis

An observed improvement can be compatible with several causal explanations when relevant conditions changed together. A later change in interpretation can leave the recorded earlier observation unchanged.

## Purpose
Connect knowledge to real use while preserving the distinction between results and interpretation.

## Representation
Reference knowledge -> choose a bounded task -> record prediction -> perform -> record observation -> compare explanations -> keep / modify / reject / unresolved -> revise the smallest affected object.

The reusable Practice remains distinct from an optional dated Practice Record. Record whether the intended action was actually executed before attributing the outcome to it.

## Assumptions
An outcome measure is suitable for the task; records contain real observations. Earlier inputs are not silently rewritten.

## Implications
Revision may concern a local cue, the chosen scope or the evidence assessment rather than the whole theory. A failed task need not invalidate every upstream object.

## Evidence and evaluation
[Re-examining the testing effect as a learning strategy: the advantage of retrieval practice over concept mapping as a methodological artifact](../references/mayrhofer-2023-reexamining-the-testing-effect.md#verbatim-performance-under-the-revised-comparison) illustrates attribution sensitivity; [Spaced retrieval: absolute spacing enhances learning regardless of relative spacing](../references/karpicke-2011-spaced-retrieval.md#absolute-and-relative-retrieval-spacing) is a scoped learning input. The operating loop is authored design.

## Limits
One person’s success does not establish a general effect. Important or hazardous outcomes can require expertise beyond this process.

## Local evidence summary


[Re-examining the testing effect as a learning strategy: the advantage of retrieval practice over concept mapping as a methodological artifact](../references/mayrhofer-2023-reexamining-the-testing-effect.md#verbatim-performance-under-the-revised-comparison) contributes: The study reports no statistically significant retrieval advantage on verbatim questions once additional memorization is included in the mapping comparison. This is a condition-specific result.

[Spaced retrieval: absolute spacing enhances learning regardless of relative spacing](../references/karpicke-2011-spaced-retrieval.md#absolute-and-relative-retrieval-spacing) contributes: In the reported experiment, expanding the total spacing between repeated retrieval attempts improved later retention; no relative spacing schedule was inherently superior in that comparison.

Citation identifies support for the stated claim and scope. Any further causal or practical inference remains separately reviewable.
