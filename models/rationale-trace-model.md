# Rationale trace model

## Key takeaway

Answer why a Method is a plausible choice without suggesting it is uniquely deduced.

## Summary

For an action that generates a summary, identify the claim “independent copies may drift,” the goal of local readability, and the convention to record dependencies. The convention is a chosen response, not the inevitable consequence of the claim.

## Local explanatory basis

Writing the premises and inference connecting a choice makes those stated assumptions available for inspection. Evidence for one population, task, comparison or outcome does not automatically establish an effect for a materially different target.

## Purpose
Answer why a Method is a plausible choice without suggesting it is uniquely deduced.

## Representation
| Element | Example question |
|---|---|
| Goal | What outcome matters? |
| Evidence-supported premises | What do the sources actually establish? |
| Principle | What reusable relationship matters? |
| Model | How is that relationship represented here? |
| Added assumptions | What must be true for this application? |
| Alternatives | What else could satisfy the goal? |
| Method | What operation do we choose to try? |
| Prediction and challenge | What would support or weaken this use? |

The chain may start from empirical guidance rather than theory. A “based on” link records dependency, not logical entailment.

## Assumptions
Goals and tolerable costs are explicit. A convention is not passed off as a finding.

## Implications
The rationale belongs beside the action. Practice-step rationale explains why here and now; Method rationale explains why the operation may help.

## Evidence and evaluation
Logical decomposition supported by [Explicit reasoning exposes assumptions](../principles/explicit-reasoning-exposes-assumptions.md). A useful test is whether another reader can name the uncertain bridge and propose a competing Method.

## Limits
No automatic chain from a discipline name to a procedure. Longer rationale can obscure rather than improve the explanation.

## Local evidence summary

This file uses KPS’s declared conventions or an explicit reasoning argument rather than claiming an externally tested intervention. Its explanation and counterexample are local; linked objects identify reusable connections, not independent confirmation.

## Deeper knowledge

Canonical dependencies and Reference records provide additional derivation, source inspection and alternatives. They are not required to carry out the local explanation or procedure.
