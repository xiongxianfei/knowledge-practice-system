# Knowledge role model

## Key takeaway

Distinguish the primary jobs of reusable knowledge without claiming that thought follows one rigid pipeline.

## Summary

Consider one recurring task: compare a source-supported claim with a proposed action. “Evidence” is a Concept; evidence applicability is a Principle; a claim–evidence table is a Model; source inspection is a Method; building the domain coordinates them as a Practice.

## Local explanatory basis

The representation below is a declared design convention. Its entities and relationships are chosen for the question, not claimed as the only structure permitted by science.

## Purpose
Distinguish the primary jobs of reusable knowledge without claiming that thought follows one rigid pipeline.

## Representation
| Role | Primary content | Does not substitute for |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Definition or distinction | Evidence of a causal claim |
| Principle | Reusable explanatory relationship | A mandatory design choice |
| Model | Structured representation | Validation of its assumptions |
| Method | Repeatable operation with rationale | Evidence that it works for everyone |
| Practice | Selection, sequencing and adaptation | A record that it was actually used |

References and Practice Records support these roles; they are not extra knowledge types. A Model can inform a Principle, and a Principle can constrain a Model, but a cycle of links is not evidence.

## Assumptions
The five roles are adopted conventions. An object can contain supporting material of other roles while retaining one primary role.

## Implications
Classify by the job of the content. Keep a local rule within the Method or Model it governs; extract an explanation only when it has independent reuse.

## Evidence and evaluation
Evaluate the convention through real retrieval, application and revision tasks. No cited research establishes five categories as optimal.

## Limits
Do not manufacture a Principle or Model to fill a folder. A Method with empirical support need not have a complete mechanistic derivation.

## Local evidence summary

This file uses KPS’s declared conventions or an explicit reasoning argument rather than claiming an externally tested intervention. Its explanation and counterexample are local; linked objects identify reusable connections, not independent confirmation.

## Deeper knowledge

Canonical dependencies and Reference records provide additional derivation, source inspection and alternatives. They are not required to carry out the local explanation or procedure.
