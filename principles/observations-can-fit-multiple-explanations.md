# Observations can fit multiple explanations

## Key takeaway

An observed improvement can be compatible with several causal explanations when relevant conditions changed together.

## Summary

Outcome change alone does not separate intervention, practice, exposure time, expectation, selection or measurement differences. A prediction makes an explanation testable but does not make it true.

## Worked use and boundary

A user finishes faster with a new Practice. Possible explanations include clearer instructions, an easier task or prior practice. The observed result is useful; the causal conclusion needs a comparison that addresses those alternatives.

## Statement
An observed improvement can be compatible with several causal explanations when relevant conditions changed together.

## Explanation
Outcome change alone does not separate intervention, practice, exposure time, expectation, selection or measurement differences. A prediction makes an explanation testable but does not make it true.

## Evidence and source synthesis
[Re-examining the testing effect as a learning strategy: the advantage of retrieval practice over concept mapping as a methodological artifact](../references/mayrhofer-2023-reexamining-the-testing-effect.md#verbatim-performance-under-the-revised-comparison) reports a design-specific comparison in which an apparent advantage disappeared after another learning activity was included. [Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, version 6.5](../references/cochrane-2024-handbook-v6-5.md#indirectness-relative-to-the-target-question) provides complementary comparison cautions.

## Derivation and applications
Comparable low-risk trials, alternative hypotheses and recording changed conditions reduce avoidable ambiguity. These are possible Methods, not a guarantee of causal identification.

## Scope
Before/after judgments and informal comparisons, including personal use of KPS.

## Limits and alternatives
Not every decision requires an experiment. A useful local choice can be made under uncertainty without claiming a universal mechanism.

## Qualification and challenge
A design that isolates alternatives more convincingly, or replicated target evidence, may justify stronger attribution.
