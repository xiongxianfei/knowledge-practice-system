# Learning effects depend on task and comparison

## Key takeaway

The effect attributed to a learning activity depends on the learning task, comparison activities and later test.

## Summary

A method label can conceal differences in restudy, memorization, retrieval or feedback even when total learning time is matched. Immediate performance, delayed recall and application are distinct outcomes.

## Worked use and boundary

For reviewing technical text, specify whether success means recalling terms, explaining relationships or applying them. Compare actual activities and feedback rather than treating “mapping” and “retrieval” as interchangeable experimental packages.

## Statement
The effect attributed to a learning activity depends on the learning task, comparison activities and later test.

## Explanation
A method label can conceal differences in restudy, memorization, retrieval or feedback even when total learning time is matched. Immediate performance, delayed recall and application are distinct outcomes.

## Evidence and source synthesis
[Spaced retrieval: absolute spacing enhances learning regardless of relative spacing](../references/karpicke-2011-spaced-retrieval.md#absolute-and-relative-retrieval-spacing) supports spaced retrieval in its reported task. [Retrieval practice produces more learning than elaborative studying with concept mapping](../references/karpicke-2011-retrieval-and-concept-mapping.md#reported-learning-comparison) reports a retrieval/mapping advantage; [Re-examining the testing effect as a learning strategy: the advantage of retrieval practice over concept mapping as a methodological artifact](../references/mayrhofer-2023-reexamining-the-testing-effect.md#verbatim-performance-under-the-revised-comparison) challenges its broad interpretation with a changed comparison. These are separate studies, not a pooled estimate. The challenge to mapping comparisons is not a disproof of spacing.

## Derivation and applications
Retrieval and later checks are reasonable candidates for knowledge retention. Application to a real work problem needs its own outcome check; physical skill is not established by recalling the instructions.

## Scope
Learning tasks comparable to the cited experiments; KPS review design is an explicit extension.

## Limits and alternatives
The original main article and the later methodological challenge were inspected; the spaced-retrieval study remains abstract-only in this review. This is a bounded comparison, not a complete review of learning research. There is no universal review schedule or claim that one activity beats all others.

## Qualification and challenge
Target-task results or better-controlled comparisons can qualify or reverse a proposed preference.

## Local evidence summary


[Spaced retrieval: absolute spacing enhances learning regardless of relative spacing](../references/karpicke-2011-spaced-retrieval.md#absolute-and-relative-retrieval-spacing) contributes: In the reported experiment, expanding the total spacing between repeated retrieval attempts improved later retention; no relative spacing schedule was inherently superior in that comparison.

[Retrieval practice produces more learning than elaborative studying with concept mapping](../references/karpicke-2011-retrieval-and-concept-mapping.md#reported-learning-comparison) contributes: The reported retrieval condition outperformed the concept-mapping condition on the study’s learning outcomes.

[Re-examining the testing effect as a learning strategy: the advantage of retrieval practice over concept mapping as a methodological artifact](../references/mayrhofer-2023-reexamining-the-testing-effect.md#verbatim-performance-under-the-revised-comparison) contributes: The study reports no statistically significant retrieval advantage on verbatim questions once additional memorization is included in the mapping comparison. This is a condition-specific result.

Citation identifies support for the stated claim and scope. Any further causal or practical inference remains separately reviewable.
