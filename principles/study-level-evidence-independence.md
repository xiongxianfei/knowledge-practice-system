# Study Level Evidence Independence

## Key takeaway

Counting documents can overstate independent evidential support.

## Summary

Cochrane requires linked reports to be considered at study level; document counts and independent investigations are different units.

## Statement

Several reports describing one investigation do not supply the same independence as separate investigations.

## Reasoning

Duplicating a description introduces no new observation by itself. Complementary reports can still add important details, so deduplication must not discard them indiscriminately.

## Example

Three synthetic documents repeat the same outcome for the same participants. Treating them as three trials would count the participants more than once. Keep the report links, extract complementary information and retain uncertainty about possible overlap.

## Limits

This is not a claim that every distinct study is independent. Shared datasets or linked samples may need additional investigation. Source independence and methodological quality are separate concerns.
