# Multiple reports can share one evidence base

## Key takeaway

The number of documents repeating a result can exceed the number of independent observations supporting it.

## Summary

A paper, press release, abstract page and later summary may all trace to one experiment. Additional documents can add detail without adding a new replication.

## Worked use and boundary

A paper, its conference abstract and three summaries may describe the same participants. Record one study family. Independent coaching organizations may also share traditions without supplying independent effectiveness trials.

## Evidence and source synthesis
[Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, version 6.5](../references/cochrane-2024-handbook-v6-5.md#reports-and-underlying-studies) explicitly distinguishes studies from reports. [PROV-Overview](../references/w3c-2013-prov-overview.md#provenance-describes-production-history) offers a way to describe derivation. The independent-document example is a logical illustration, not measured KPS data.

## Derivation and applications
Recording an evidence family allows comparison by underlying contribution rather than headline count. The same approach can detect shared authorship or advice lineage, although independence may remain unknown.

## Scope
Research synthesis, coaching claims and summaries with common upstream sources.

## Limits and alternatives
Shared authorship does not prove dependence, and different publishers do not prove independence. Secondary reports can contain unique corrections or outcomes.

## Qualification and challenge
Evidence of genuinely separate sampling, experiments or independently developed reasoning changes the family assessment.

## Local evidence summary


[Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, version 6.5](../references/cochrane-2024-handbook-v6-5.md#reports-and-underlying-studies) contributes: Several reports can describe one underlying study; report count is therefore not study count.

[PROV-Overview](../references/w3c-2013-prov-overview.md#provenance-describes-production-history) contributes: PROV represents the origins of entities through activities and agents, enabling provenance to be exchanged.
