# Model

## Key takeaway

A Model represents a system for a question; its assumptions are part of the representation.

## Summary

A Model connects defined elements through relationships. It may be a diagram, equation, state transition or structured verbal account. It supports interpretation or prediction only at its chosen level of detail and under its assumptions.

## Definition and use

A purposeful representation of entities, relationships, assumptions and sometimes predictions for a stated question.

A claim–evidence map contains a source passage, a supported premise, an additional assumption and a proposed action. A line between nodes records a relation; it does not make the inference valid. Inspect both the nodes and the reason for the edge.

## Boundary and non example

Distinguish a model’s chosen constraints from externally established relationships. A useful simplified model need not describe everything, but its omissions must not hide decision-critical limitations.
