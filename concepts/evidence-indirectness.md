# Evidence Indirectness

## Key takeaway

Evidence can concern a different question from the decision at hand.

## Summary

Indirectness is a GRADE consideration described in Cochrane guidance, not a synonym for low quality in every respect.

## Meaning

Differences between the studied and intended population, intervention, comparison or outcome can make evidence indirect. Certainty also depends on other considerations; indirectness alone does not produce a complete rating.

## Example

A synthetic review asks about long-term functioning, while an available study reports only a short-term surrogate. It may remain relevant, but an additional argument is needed to use it for the intended outcome.
