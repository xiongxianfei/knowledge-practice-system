# Method

## Key takeaway

A Method provides an executable operation, its reason, and a way to evaluate the result.

## Summary

A Method is a repeatable operation such as checking source support, comparing two safe alternatives or updating a document. It states inputs, prerequisites, steps, output, rationale and failure conditions. “Repeatable” means the procedure can be repeated, not that success is guaranteed.

## Definition and use

A repeatable operation for investigating, deciding, constructing, testing or revising something.

To check a claim, write the claim, inspect the cited passage, compare scope, then record supported / limited / contradicted / unknown. A Practice decides when that checking operation is needed during a larger task.

## Boundary and non example

A Method may have direct empirical support, a logical basis or an explicit design hypothesis. Its rationale is not evidence that it has been tested.
