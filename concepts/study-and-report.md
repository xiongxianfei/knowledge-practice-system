# Study and Report

## Key takeaway

One study can produce several reports.

## Summary

Cochrane distinguishes the investigation from the documents describing it. That distinction matters before evidence is counted or extracted.

## Meaning

A study is the investigation of interest; a report is a source describing some part of it. The relationships can be many-to-many. A conference report and later article may contain complementary rather than independent findings.

## Example

Synthetic records A and B share a trial identifier and participants. Record C is a press release about A. These are leads to one investigation, not automatically three confirmations. If linkage is uncertain, leave that uncertainty explicit.

## Boundary

This is the review-methods meaning, not a universal definition of every type of evidence. A standards definition is not a clinical study. Different report dates or websites do not establish independence.
