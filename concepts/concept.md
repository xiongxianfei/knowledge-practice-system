# Concept

## Key takeaway

A Concept defines a distinction; it does not establish a causal claim.

## Summary

A Concept makes a term usable: it explains what belongs in a category, what does not, and why confusing it with a neighbouring idea would matter. In KPS, a file defining “Method” is a Concept even though its subject is another knowledge type.

## Definition and use

A definition or distinction used to identify what a domain is talking about.

Use a definition and a contrasting case. “Evidence” is information relevant to a claim; “a famous publication” identifies a possible source, not its evidentiary contribution. A statement that explains how evidence behaves belongs elsewhere when independently reusable.

## Boundary and non example

A definition may be stipulated for one system or reported from a source. Identify which; neither kind automatically proves that its category is the only sensible one.
