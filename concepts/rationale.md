# Rationale

## Key takeaway

Rationale exposes the bridge from knowledge and goals to a selected action.

## Summary

Rationale is the reasoning inside a Method or Practice step: given a supported premise, additional assumptions and a goal, why is this action a reasonable candidate? It makes “why this, here?” inspectable. It is a section, not a sixth core knowledge type.

## Definition and use

The written reasoning that connects a goal, evidence, assumptions and knowledge to a proposed action or selection.

Copies can diverge; these summaries are meant to remain consistent; therefore record their dependencies and review them after a source changes. That argument proposes maintenance. It does not show that a hash check can judge semantic accuracy.

## Boundary and non example

Separate a source’s conclusion from your inference. A plausible rationale can coexist with weak evidence; say so and specify the observation that would change the choice.
