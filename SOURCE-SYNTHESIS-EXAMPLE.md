# Worked source synthesis retrieval and concept mapping

## Key takeaway

Compare actual learning activities and outcomes before ranking the method labels.

## Summary

This bounded desk synthesis compares the 2011 Karpicke–Blunt main article with the 2023 Mayrhofer and colleagues re-examination. It also distinguishes a separate spaced-retrieval study. It illustrates source synthesis; it is not an exhaustive literature review or an evaluation of KPS.

## Question and boundary

Does the inspected evidence establish that retrieval is always better than concept mapping for using a knowledge system? No such universal conclusion is warranted by this comparison. The evidence concerns specified learning activities and tests, not a file architecture or engineering decision workflow.

## Agreement disagreement and independence

The first two are different experiments, not duplicated reports. They share a research question but differ in activities. The later authors argue that memorization opportunities affect interpretation. The original authors explicitly matched **total learning time**. Therefore “one group simply had twice the total study time” is not an accurate account of the original main article.

The absence of a statistically significant advantage in one comparison is not proof of exact equality or universal interchangeability. The inference outcome needs additional caution because of its small question set. The separately inspected spacing abstract concerns a different question.

## Source supported premises and authored inference

The inspected studies show that learning activity details and the later test matter to the reported comparison. We infer that a KPS review should state its target—recall, explanation or application—and examine those outcomes separately. We do not infer that five folders, more links, or self-contained files automatically improve retention.

A candidate operation is to attempt an explanation from memory, compare it with the source, correct misunderstandings, and later apply it to a new task. That is an authored review procedure with relevant empirical inputs, not a claim that these studies tested this exact procedure.

## Search and access limits

This is a selected original-versus-challenge comparison, not a complete search of retrieval and mapping research. The original main article was inspected through the authors’ PDF, including visual inspection of printed page 773; supplementary materials and raw data were not audited. The re-examination was read in full HTML. The spacing source was inspected at abstract level. No effect-size synthesis or independent reanalysis was performed.

## Decision and next check

Keep the Principle scoped to tasks and comparison conditions. For actual knowledge work, separately check delayed explanation and practical application. No such user experiment has been conducted by this publication.
